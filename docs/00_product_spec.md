# URPP V0 Product Specification

## Product Thesis

URPP is not primarily a chatbot.

URPP is a persistent learning-control loop for one university STEM
student taking one active course.

## North Star

> Given the course requirement and current student evidence,
> what is the next best teaching action?

## Target User

One university STEM student enrolled in an active course whose
materials arrive over time.

## V0 Must Demonstrate

1. New course material changes the Course Model.
2. Course requirements become explicit Learning Objectives.
3. Student-state claims are evidence-backed.
4. Student-state claims are traceable.
5. Student state may rise, decline, or remain unknown.
6. Teaching actions are selected from a constrained action space.
7. Teaching actions have explicit reason codes.
8. Teaching is followed by assessment.
9. Assessment produces Evidence Events.
10. Evidence changes future teaching behavior.
11. URPP exposes uncertainty.
12. URPP is compared with simpler baselines.
