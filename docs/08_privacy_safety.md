# Privacy and Student-Model Safety

URPP should model observable learning behavior.

URPP should not infer unnecessary:

- medical conditions
- mental-health conditions
- IQ
- personality types
- fixed learning-style labels

Avoid:

> You are a visual learner.

Prefer:

> In the last four sessions, worked examples followed by independent
> practice were associated with fewer hints and better transfer.

Students should eventually be able to inspect and dispute URPP claims.
